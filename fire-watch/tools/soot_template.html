Soot & haze · smoke particles · MERRA-2 (NASA)

How much smoke-type aerosol is in the air, when, and does it follow fire?

Pick a measure: black carbon (soot, the sooty particles from burning), PM2.5 (fine particles of all kinds) or aerosol optical depth (how hazy the whole air column is). All three come from NASA's MERRA-2 reanalysis (the Giovanni files already in Climate Watch), averaged over a box covering Cambodia and parts of Thailand and Vietnam, monthly from January 2001 to December 2024. Fire counts come from NASA FIRMS (the Fire tab). Choose which fire seasons to look at; the satellite switch only changes the fire side of the comparison.
