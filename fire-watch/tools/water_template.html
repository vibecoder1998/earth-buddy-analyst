Rain, soil & heat · GPM IMERG + MERRA-2 (NASA)

Is the water and heat of the Cambodia box changing, and is the change real?

Five monthly series, January 2001 to December 2024, each averaged over one box (102.3–107.6°E, 10.4–14.7°N, which also takes in parts of Thailand and Vietnam): rain from the GPM satellites, and rain, soil wetness, air temperature and wet-bulb temperature from NASA's MERRA-2 reanalysis. Pick a series and a range of years; the rate of change, its 95% confidence interval and the significance tests all recompute. Section 3 compares the two rain records, because they disagree.
